Elicitation Defense Lab

A grounded self-assessment of how conversational elicitation techniques land on you, and how well you would recognize, counter, and report them. It is not a validated psychometric instrument. Treat the scores as a structured prompt for reflection and training, not a measurement.

Everything runs in your browser. There is no backend, no analytics, and no transmission of any kind. Your answers are scored locally and never leave the page. In-progress answers are kept only in this browser's local storage so a reload does not lose them.


Self-assessment

Rate each statement from 0 (strongly disagree) to 4 (strongly agree).

Interaction indicators

Check every indicator you noticed in the conversation you are reviewing.

Knowledge check

For each scenario, pick the recognition, countermeasure, and reporting response you would use.
